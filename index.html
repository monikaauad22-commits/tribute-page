Dr. Norman Borlaug

The Father of the Green Revolution

Dr. Norman Borlaug, an agricultural scientist whose work helped fight world hunger.

His Work and Legacy

Dr. Norman Borlaug was an American agricultural scientist, humanitarian, and Nobel Peace Prize laureate.

He dedicated his life to improving agricultural production and developing disease-resistant, high-yield crops.

His research and leadership played an important role in increasing food production in developing countries and helping millions of people avoid starvation.

Why He Is Remembered

  • Developed high-yield, disease-resistant wheat varieties.
  • Helped expand modern agricultural techniques around the world.
  • Contributed significantly to the Green Revolution.
  • Received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1970.
  • Continued working to improve global food security.
